## Project title

Auditable AI-assisted exact computation for higher moments in Riemann zeta research

## Short summary

We request a $1,000 API-credit pilot to investigate higher-moment candidates in research on simple zeros of the Riemann zeta function. The project combines exact rational computation, independent checks, and explicit tracking of unresolved proof obligations. Existing code reproduces the moment-consumption algebra and target inequalities. The immediate task is to certify or refute a candidate four-dimensional piecewise-polynomial integral. We will report both accepted certificates and failed attempts, with reproducible artifacts and actual resource usage. No new unconditional zeta-zero record is claimed.

## Research question and significance

Can AI-assisted exact computation resolve a higher-moment bottleneck while maintaining a clear boundary between numerical conjecture, verified finite computation, and analytic proof?

The long-term mathematical objective is to improve lower bounds for simple critical-line zeros. The pilot focuses on the next falsifiable calculation, rather than promising the eventual theorem. A successful exact value, rigorous enclosure, or counterexample would clarify which higher-moment routes remain viable and give a useful example of auditable AI assistance in research mathematics.

## Existing work and evidence

The private repository contains exact Christoffel/Hankel consumption, seventh/eighth-moment target geometry, and a research handoff that enumerates certification and transport gaps. Three baseline Python scripts passed in the preparation run. An additional independent spectator-reduction check passed 7,203 exact rational cases.

The higher-moment analytic framework is inherited from the reference implementation identified in HANDOFF.md. Its assumptions and conventions require independent review. We do not claim to have established that framework, a new unconditional record, or a proof of the Riemann hypothesis.

The repository's retirement of an early `{5,2}=1/8` numerical candidate after exact spectator-frequency elimination illustrates why the proposed verification gates matter. The current `7/72` replacement remains unproved. A numerical reconstruction is not an acceptable theorem input.

## Planned use of Claude

We propose using Claude to help derive cell decompositions, implement rational polynomial integration, produce independent derivations, challenge normalizations, and organize analytic proof obligations. Outputs must pass exact checks or explicit proof review before acceptance. This is a planned use; we do not attribute earlier research to Claude without records establishing that attribution.

The initial pilot would last six weeks. It would deliver a pinned specification, integration certificates or a documented obstruction, a candidate decision, a transport-obligation ledger, and a reproduction/resource report. AI suggestions do not replace human mathematical review. The project must arrange an independent reviewer; no reviewer is yet committed.

## Requested support and evaluation

Requested support: $1,000 of first-party API credits. Proposed allocation: $400 integration implementation/debugging; $300 independent derivations and falsification; $200 transport analysis; $100 reproduction and reporting. Smaller support would fund a correspondingly reduced scope. Exact runtime, model mix, and token requirements will be measured in the pilot rather than presented as known.

We will track model identifiers, usage, cost, attempts, accepted certificates, anchor recovery, rejected candidates, and unresolved obligations. An increase would be justified only by demonstrated progress. Credit support is distinct from cash support and subscription access.
